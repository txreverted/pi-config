# Unslop

Write like a person. Keep the meaning and match the intended tone.

## Method

1. Find the patterns below.
2. Rewrite in plain language.
3. Add a specific human voice where it fits.
4. Ask, "What makes this look generated?" Fix what remains.

## Voice

- Have an opinion when the facts support one.
- Vary sentence length. Short is good. A longer sentence can carry nuance.
- Admit real complexity instead of flattening every trade-off.
- Use "I" when it sounds natural.
- Prefer concrete details over generic reactions.

## Remove these patterns

1. Cut puffery such as "pivotal moment", "testament to", "evolving landscape", and "indelible mark".
2. Do not list famous sources without saying what each source adds.
3. Remove shallow `-ing` phrases such as "highlighting", "ensuring", and "showcasing". State the fact instead.
4. Replace promotional words such as "vibrant", "breathtaking", "groundbreaking", and "must-visit" with neutral facts.
5. Name the source behind a claim. Delete vague phrases such as "experts believe".
6. Replace "despite challenges, it continues to thrive" with the specific problem and result.
7. Avoid stock AI words: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, foster, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.
8. Prefer "is" and "has" over "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", and "features".
9. Replace "not just X, but Y" with the actual point.
10. Do not force ideas into groups of three.
11. Pick one term for one thing. Do not cycle through synonyms.
12. Use a range only when its endpoints share a real scale.
13. Avoid em dashes. Split the sentence or use a comma.
14. Use colons for lists and examples, not as a dramatic connector.
15. Use bold only where it helps scanning.
16. Avoid list items whose bold label merely repeats the sentence.
17. Use sentence case headings.
18. Remove decorative emoji.
19. Use straight quotes.
20. Remove chatbot filler such as "Of course", "Great question", "I hope this helps", and "Let me know if".
21. Do not excuse missing facts. Find a source or remove the claim.
22. Do not flatter the user instead of answering.
23. Shorten filler: "in order to" becomes "to", "due to the fact that" becomes "because".
24. Use one honest hedge when uncertainty matters. Remove stacked hedges.
25. End with a concrete fact or action, not a generic hopeful conclusion.
26. Replace abstract technical metaphors such as substrate, wedge, vector, nexus, primitive, harness, surface, bedrock, scaffolding, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet, and north star with the real mechanism.
27. Say what something does. Prefer "`.toSQL()` returns the sent SQL" over "SQL stays close at hand".
28. Split dense sentences. Keep one main idea per sentence.
29. Prefer active voice. Name the actor when it matters.
30. Cut adverbs that prop up weak verbs. Use the measured result when one exists.
31. Prefer the plain word: use, help, many, and if. Avoid utilize, leverage, facilitate, numerous, and "in the event that".
